Correctness and complexity

Greedy DSatur: repeatedly selects the unassigned course with the most differently coloured neighbours, then assigns the first legal slot with capacity remaining. It produces a valid timetable but does not guarantee the minimum number of slots.

Exact backtracking: tries slot limits in increasing order and searches for a valid assignment under the conflict and hall-capacity constraints. The first feasible limit is optimal if the search completes. A time-limited result is explicitly marked as not proven optimal.

Complexity: greedy is polynomial (roughly O(N² + M) in this implementation). Exact graph colouring is exponential in the worst case.
