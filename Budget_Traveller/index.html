Algorithm and correctness

State: dp[f][city][usedCoupons] stores minimum cost to reach a city using exactly f flights and that many coupons.

  • At most K stops means at most K+1 flights.
  • Each flight transition considers full price or floor(price/2) with one coupon.
  • The minimum over all allowed destination states is optimal because every valid route and coupon choice is represented.
  • Time: O((K+1)M(C+1)); space: O((K+1)N(C+1)).
Corner cases
  • Unreachable destination → -1.
  • N=1 → cost 0.
  • Odd prices, zero prices, unused coupons, parallel flights and cycles.
  • Negative prices are rejected.
